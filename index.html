You've seen it. Job sites buried in ads. Postings that vanish into a black hole. Recruiters blasting the same message to thousands. Resumes that flatten years of work into a single page nobody reads.

Build your Stack

Every project you create or join lands on your Stack, with credit from the people you built it with. Highlight what you're proudest of, hide what you've outgrown, and let your work do the talking.
